### 💡 Tips for a Helpful Bug Report

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please consider including:
* **Step-by-step details:** What were you doing right before the bug occurred?
* **Screenshots:** A visual screenshot of the error screen or unexpected text is extremely helpful.
* **App Version:** You can find your current version at the very bottom of the **Settings** screen in LinguoBook.
