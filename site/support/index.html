    Support Center & FAQ

    Welcome to the LinguoBook Support Center! Here you will find answers to the most frequently asked questions regarding subscriptions, importing books, translations, the personal dictionary, and progress tracking.

    💳 Subscription & Billing

    How does the LinguoBook subscription work and what is included in Pro?

    The LinguoBook Premium subscription unlocks: * No ads. * Unlimited access to all app features.

    How can I cancel or change my subscription plan?

    You can manage your subscription at any time: 1. Open the Google Play Store or Apple App Store on your device. 2. Go to the Subscriptions section. 3. Select LinguoBook and tap Cancel Subscription or change your plan.

    After canceling, you will retain full access to Pro features until the end of your current billing cycle.

    📚 Book Import & Catalog

    What file formats can I import into the reader?

    LinguoBook natively supports the following formats: * .EPUB, .MOBI, .FB2, .TXT, and .ZIP

    ⏳ Coming Soon: Full support for .PDF files will arrive in an upcoming update!

    Where can I get free books directly inside the app?

    You don't need to bring your own files! The app includes an integrated Standard EBooks catalog, allowing you to download thousands of beautifully formatted public domain classics in English with a single tap. Find the cloud button on your Bookshelf screen.

    🌍 Translations & Personal Dictionary

    Which languages are supported and how does the translator work?

    LinguoBook supports instant translations and Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio, synonyms, part of speech and definitions for 8 languages: * 🇬🇧 English * 🇵🇱 Polish * 🇺🇦 Ukrainian * 🇨🇿 Czech * 🇩🇪 German * 🇪🇸 Spanish * 🇮🇹 Italian * 🇫🇷 French

    How do I save words to my dictionary?

    While reading, simply tap any word or select a phrase. In the translation pop-up, tap the Save icon (bookmark/plus) to add the word to your Personal Dictionary. You can review and reinforce saved words anytime in the context of the exact sentences where you found them.

    📊 Statistics & Daily Streak

    How does the habit tracker (Daily Streak) work?

    Every day you read at least a short excerpt, another day is added to your Daily Streak.

    The app automatically tracks your progress and monitors: * 📈 Total number of translated words. * 📚 Completed books. * 🔥 Current and longest daily reading streaks.
